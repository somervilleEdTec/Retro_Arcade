## Re-import instructions (local)

1. Download packs listed in `docs` design-plan asset tables (Kenney UI, Beat em Up Graphics Pack, Craftpix free city enemies).
2. Drop PNGs into `assets/` mirroring the folder layout; keep **Nearest** filter (project default).
3. Recolor any SoR-lookalike sheets before public builds.
